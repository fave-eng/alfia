Speakout A1 · Unit 1B

Jobs: talking about people

To describe another person, use he / she + is. To describe a thing or a place, use it + is. Study this grammar separately from articles:

She's a nurse.Она медсестра.
He's an artist.Он художник.

For the full explanation, including questions and negative sentences, use the grammar section. Jobs have their own vocabulary category inside Lesson 2.

Grammar · Lesson 1B · separate topic

Articles: a / an

Use a before a consonant sound: a nurse, a waiter. Use an before a vowel sound: an actor, an office worker.

Compare She’s a doctor (a profession: singular countable noun) with She’s British (nationality adjective, no article).
